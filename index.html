❓ Pixel Fit Help & About

🎨 Pixel Fit v1.0 — Virtual Try-On

Welcome to Pixel Fit, a nostalgic Y2K Windows 98 / MS Paint dressing room!

  • Drag & Drop or Click: Pick any clothing item from the Wardrobe Tray and drag it onto the mannequin, or click it to auto-equip.
  • Resize & Rotate: Click placed items to reveal handles. Drag the bottom corner square to scale, and drag the yellow top circle to spin!
  • Keyboard Controls: Use Arrow keys to nudge placed items (hold Shift for 10px jumps). Press Delete to remove.
  • 🎲 Randomize: Choose a style tag (Streetwear, Y2K Cyber, Grunge, etc.) and hit Randomize for an instant coordinated fit.
  • Doodle Layer: Use the Pencil, Brush, Airbrush, or Eraser tools from the MS Paint toolbar to draw right on your fit!
  • Save & Export: Click the red Save Fit button to export a crisp PNG image and save your look into your wardrobe gallery.

Built with pure HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript. 100% self-contained with real-time Web Audio API sound synthesis.
